A Place of Quiet

YUME is a spacious, single-story private villa of approximately 180㎡, with three Japanese-style rooms and one Western-style bedroom. Wake to birdsong, spend the day among the trees, and end the evening beneath a star-filled sky.

Time at YUME

Enjoy a generous living and dining space, Japanese and Western-style rooms, a fully equipped kitchen, and the gentle lights of the garden at night.

Easy Parking

Free parking for up to five cars.
Ample parking is available close to the villa.

Access

About 8 minutes by car from Izukyu-Shimoda Station (about 3.5km).

Enjoy the Area

Beautiful beaches such as Yumigahama, Kisami Ohama, Touji, and Tatado are nearby. Shimoda Aquarium and the town of Shimoda are also easy to reach for a day of swimming, sightseeing, or dining.
