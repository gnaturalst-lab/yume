THE STORY OF YUME

A quietness found
only here.

YUME is a private villa surrounded by the rich nature of Minami-Izu. The spacious single-story house offers approximately 180 square meters, with three Japanese-style rooms and one Western-style bedroom. Wake to birdsong, watch the garden lights at night, and enjoy the luxury of doing nothing.

A QUIET NIGHT

As night deepens,
quietness fills the air.

Soft lights illuminate the trees beneath the clear Minami-Izu sky. The garden takes on a different character after dark, becoming part of the experience of staying at YUME.

JAPANESE ROOM

Rest on tatami,
the Japanese way.

A calm Japanese-style room surrounded by painted fusuma panels. Futons can be arranged for families and groups of friends to sleep comfortably.

STAY & DETAILS

Time at YUME

A spacious dining room and kitchen, a calm Western-style bedroom, and furniture and tableware that have been carefully kept over the years. The comfort of this house comes not only from what is new, but also from what has been preserved.
